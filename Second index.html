What is BaaS File?

Banking-as-a-service (BaaS) is the practice of banking systems allowing nonbank third-party businesses to offer their banking or financial services to customers through application programming interfaces (APIs). BaaS act as intermediaries between traditional banks and companies that want to integrate banking services—such as payment processing, lending, or issuing debit and credit cards—into their offerings.
